Why following up matters

Social media and online ads make it easy to get leads. Keeping up with all of them is the hard part. Most business owners reach out once, get no answer, and move on to the next name on the list.

That silence usually doesn't mean no. It means busy, distracted, or not ready yet. A clear follow-up system keeps you in front of people until the timing is right, and it makes your customers feel remembered instead of forgotten.

Day 1

Hey Jordan, thanks for stopping by the shop today. Want me to hold a spot for you next Saturday?

Day 3

Just checking in. I've still got a 10am and a 2pm open Saturday if either works.

Day 7

Last one from me this week. Should I keep you on the list for next month instead?

Sorry, crazy week! Put me down for the 2pm
