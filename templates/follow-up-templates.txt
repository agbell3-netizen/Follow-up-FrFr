FOLLOW-UP FR FR - TEMPLATE PACK
https://agbell3-netizen.github.io/Follow-up-FrFr/frameworks.html

Swap the [brackets] for your own details. Keep every message short,
give a reason, and end with one easy question.

------------------------------------------------------------
EMAIL
------------------------------------------------------------
Day 1 - Thank you
Subject: Thanks for reaching out, [Name]
Hi [Name], thanks for asking about [service]. Here's the
[quote/info] I promised. Any questions so far?

Day 3 - Something helpful
Subject: Your quote, plus one quick idea
Hi [Name], a lot of my customers start with [smaller option] to
see if it's a good fit, so I added that to your quote too. Would
you like to try it this month or next?

Day 7 - Either-or question
Subject: Quick question
Hi [Name], would [day] or [day] work better to get started?

Day 14 - Last message
Subject: Should I close your file?
Hi [Name], I haven't heard back, so I'll assume the timing isn't
right. If anything changes, just reply to this email and I'll
pick it right back up.

------------------------------------------------------------
TEXT MESSAGE
------------------------------------------------------------
Day 1
Hey [Name], it's [Your name] from [Business]. Great meeting you
today. Let me know if any questions come up about [topic].

Day 3
Hey [Name], I've still got a [time] and a [time] open this
[day]. Want me to hold one for you?

Day 7
Last one from me this week. Should I check back with you next
month instead?

------------------------------------------------------------
PHONE
------------------------------------------------------------
Opener
Hey [Name], it's [Your name]. You asked about [service] last
week, and I wanted to see if you still had questions.

Voicemail (under 20 seconds)
Hey [Name], it's [Your name] with [Business]. You asked about
[service], and I have [opening/offer]. Call or text me at
[number]. Again, that's [number]. Talk soon.

------------------------------------------------------------
IN PERSON
------------------------------------------------------------
At checkout
You're all set. Want me to save you a spot on [date]? I'll text
you a reminder the day before.

Next visit
Hey [Name]! How did [personal detail] go?
